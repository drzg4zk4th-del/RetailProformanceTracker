This page compares Pick and Pack units at the individual job/order level. Pick and Pack remain separate activities. If multiple people worked on the same job, their units are combined for that job before the comparison is made.

What the difference means

Orders / Jobs with a Pick vs Pack Difference

Positive difference = more Pack units than Pick units recorded for the job. Negative difference = more Pick units than Pack units recorded. Zero = Pick and Pack quantities match.
